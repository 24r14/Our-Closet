Our Closet

A student-led community closet providing clothing and everyday essentials at Northview Elementary in Muncie, Indiana.

Explore the results →

Program: Muncie Youth Leadership Program, Shafer Leadership Academy
School partner: Northview Elementary School
Team: Six students from three local schools
Portfolio author: Zaria Yin

Purpose and approach

The project’s mission was to provide elementary students with adequate and appropriate clothing so they could feel confident and comfortable at school. The team collected clothing, shoes, school supplies, lunchboxes, and personal care items and delivered the materials to Northview Elementary’s nurse’s office.

My contributions

I organized a donation collection at my own school and personally contacted Ball State’s Cardinal Closet to explore possible support. When the first school had no room for additional donations, I contacted another school. I also helped run the project’s social media, designed posters, and sorted donated items. These responsibilities supported the team’s outreach and donation preparation. The outreach correspondence records the follow-up and capacity issue. Selected posters and outreach examples are included in the portfolio. The collection, delivery, and first-place group presentation were shared team achievements.

How the project worked

  1. Outreach: Contacted schools and businesses, partnered with Northview, and visited the available space.
  2. Collection: Used flyers, Instagram, and signs around Ball State’s campus to invite community donations.
  3. Preparation: Divided logistics and purchases across the team; sorted clothing by type and size and gathered other essentials.
  4. Delivery: Transported collected items to the nurse’s office for access through the school.

My outreach to Ball State’s Cardinal Closet explored guidance and possible assistance with donations.

Reported collection totals

14 bags of clothing and 8 totes of shoes and other items. Personal care items included deodorant, shampoo, and soap; a separate toiletries count was not recorded on the original website.
